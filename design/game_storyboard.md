## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Escape the Cursed Jungle

**Storyline:**

You are an explorer who becomes trapped in a mysterious jungle while searching for a lost temple. The jungle is controlled by the Jungle Guardian, who will not allow anyone to escape. To survive, you must travel through the jungle and collect six important items before entering the Guardian's temple. If you enter the temple without the six items, the Guardian catches you and you lose. If you collect all six items first, you defeat the Guardian and escape the jungle.

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Jungle Entrance: Start room
2. Bamboo forest
3. Hidden Waterfall
4. Monkey Grove
5. Ancient Ruins
6. Crocodile Swamp
7. Jaguar Cave
8. Guardian's Temple

Add more rooms if your design needs them.

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Machete
2. Water Flask
3. Rope
4. Keychain
5. Hibiscus Flower
6. Torch

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

The Jungle Guardian is a powerful and mysterious creature that protects the ancient temple. It tries to stop explorers from escaping the jungle. The player must collect all six items before facing the Guardian or they will lose the game.
